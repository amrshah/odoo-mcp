# VetCairn Clinical

Clinical encounter foundation for Odoo 19 Community. It adds a daily census,
triage and vital signs, SOAP notes, encounter completion/locking, patient clinical
history, and automatic encounter creation when a visit starts.

The module uses VetCairn's technician, veterinarian, and administrator roles.
Clinical notes can be locked after completion; locked business content is
immutable. Diagnoses, procedures, prescriptions, diagnostics, vaccinations,
documents, and signed amendments remain separate later modules.
